# SAP Concur target research

Checked 14 October 2026. New registry family: expense claims. Industry: professional firms.

Annual bill: SAP Concur's own pricing page ([concur.com/about/pricing](https://www.concur.com/about/pricing)) lists Concur Expense Base "starting at $7 per report" and Plus "starting at $11 per report", both with unlimited users, and Premium on custom pricing. "Price can vary based on monthly commitment" and add-ons are quoted. The page names no currency; it is the US site. The Australian site sends small businesses to its sales team for a quote. A firm filing hundreds of reports a month pays for every one, plus card feeds, audit services and implementation; no customer invoice was checked, so the page uses the per-report prices only.

Scores: annual bill 3, data-shaped 5, buyer fit 4, search demand 4, one-session buildable 4, import path 4. Total 24/30. Rivals considered: Expensify, Emburse, Weel, Pleo, Ramp.

Import: Concur Expense Standard has an Expense File Export (a default GL file or a custom layout) produced when batches close, and larger customers buy Extract Services or use the Standard Accounting Extract, whose layout is defined per company. The import reads the common headings and takes a mapping file for the rest.

The scope is claims and their weekly work: lines, receipts and declarations, GST by country and the document rules behind GST credits, entertainment records, mileage, daily limits, approvals with limits and delegation, card matching, duplicates and the reimbursement batch file. It excludes card issuing, payments, travel booking, receipt scanning and any phone app.
