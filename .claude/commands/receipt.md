---
description: Attach a receipt to a line, or record a declaration when the receipt is lost.
---

Run: `npm run expense -- receipt <line-id> --receipt=<where it is kept> [--evidence=] --actor=`

A declaration is --declare="<what happened>" and is recorded with the person who made it. Receipts change only before approval.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Show money with its currency, GST included unless stated. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here. Nothing here pays anyone.
