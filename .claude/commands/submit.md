Run: `npm run expense -- submit <claim> --actor=`

It stops on lines over the receipt threshold with no receipt or declaration, mileage with no km, and entertainment without attendees, a head count and a purpose. List the lines to fix.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Show money with its currency, GST included unless stated. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here. Nothing here pays anyone.
