---
description: The expense categories, their accounts and rules.
---

Run: `npm run expense -- categories`

Entertainment categories need attendees and a business purpose. Mileage is claimed in km. Daily limits are per person per day.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Show money with its currency, GST included unless stated. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here. Nothing here pays anyone.
